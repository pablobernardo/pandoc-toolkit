# Fog

Imaginary fog arrives without weather and leaves without an apology. It
does not reduce visibility; it reduces certainty, which is harder to
measure and easier to enjoy.

A walker inside the fog can still see their own hands, their own notes,
and the last sentence they believed. Everything past that belongs to the
fog until it chooses to give it back.

The only recommended response is to keep writing. The fog respects a
moving pen and parts around it, exactly far enough to read by.
