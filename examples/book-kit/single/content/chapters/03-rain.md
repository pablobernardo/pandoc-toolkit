# Rain

Imaginary rain falls upward in the early pages and downward once the
reader stops checking. Each drop carries one borrowed word from a real
forecast, which is why the rain always sounds slightly familiar.

Puddles form on schedule and reflect a sky that was never photographed.
Children are advised to step in every one of them; the guide offers no
scientific grounds for this advice and stands by it completely.

When the rain ends, the air smells of clean paper. Field guides are
printed on that paper, which is how the weather recruits its own
documentarians.
