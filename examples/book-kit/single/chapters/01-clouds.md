# Clouds

Imaginary clouds are classified by what they promise rather than by what
they are made of. The most common kind drifts at reading height and
promises nothing at all, which makes it the most reliable cloud in the
sky.

Observers who need a cloud to mean something should wait for afternoon,
when the inventory thickens and shapes begin to argue with each other.

## Recording a Sighting

Write down the hour, the shape, and the promise. If the cloud keeps the
promise, the sighting was real; if it does not, the sighting was
typical. Both entries belong in the ledger, and neither one is wrong.
