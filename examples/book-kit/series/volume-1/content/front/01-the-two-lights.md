# The Two Lights

Two towers stand at the mouth of the same bay, one on each headland,
close enough to signal and too far to shout. The keepers of the north
tower and the south tower have corresponded by lantern code for eleven
years, and the code has grown to four hundred signals, of which three
hundred and ninety are about the weather.

The cycle this volume begins follows both towers through one year of
weather that neither keeper predicted and both recorded anyway.
