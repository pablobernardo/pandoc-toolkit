# The North Road

The road to the north tower climbs for two hours and argues with the
wind for both of them. The new keeper walked it in March with a
suitcase, a barometer and a letter of appointment signed by someone who
had never seen the sea.

At the top of the road the tower appeared exactly as the letter had not
described it: taller, colder, and already lit.

## The Register

Inside the door stood the register, the nightly log of the station. The
last entry was four days old and read only: fog, thick, singing. The
new keeper read it three times and decided the singing could wait until
she had unpacked the barometer.

## First Signal

At dusk the south tower lit its lamp twice, paused, and lit it twice
again. The code book said this meant is the road still open. The new
keeper opened her lantern shutter once and hoped it meant yes.
