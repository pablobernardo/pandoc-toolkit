# The Keeper

By summer the new keeper had a routine, a code-book margin full of
corrections, and a correspondent she had never met. The lantern code
had grown by two signals: one for the barometer is lying, and one for
come and see this, which they used more than either expected.

On the last night of the volume, both towers burned steady through a
storm that arrived early and left late, and in the morning the register
of the north tower read, for the first time in eleven years: weather
unchanged, correspondence continuing.
