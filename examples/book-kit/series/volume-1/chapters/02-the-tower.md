# The Tower

The tower had one hundred and nine steps, a lamp room, a kitchen, and
opinions. The stairs creaked in a sequence the keepers learned as a
tune; the kitchen window stuck in April and unstuck itself in October
without assistance.

The lamp itself was well behaved. It wanted oil, a clean chimney and
one honest hour of maintenance a day, and in exchange it drew its beam
across the water without once asking what the weather intended.

## The Singing Fog

The fog arrived on the fourth night and kept the register's promise. It
did not howl and it did not whisper; it sang, low and steady, like a
choir that had forgotten the words but remembered the key. The new
keeper wrote: fog, thick, singing, confirmed.
