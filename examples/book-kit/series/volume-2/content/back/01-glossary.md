# Glossary

**Lantern code** - the signals the two towers exchange by shutter and
lamp. Four hundred and one as of this volume; the margin corrections
are canon.

**The crossing** - the supply boat's run between the towers when the
bay allows it. Oil first, bread second, sealed notes third.

**Signal 401** - the four hundred and first code entry, added after one
week under the same roof. Its meaning is recorded in both registers and
disagreed upon in both margins.
