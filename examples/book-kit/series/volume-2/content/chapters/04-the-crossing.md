# The Crossing

Winter closed the north road in the second week of December, and the
correspondence between the towers moved to the water. The supply boat
ran when the bay allowed it, which was rarely, and each crossing
carried oil, bread, and sealed notes written in lantern code because
neither keeper trusted the post with the weather.

The new keeper of the north tower boarded for the first crossing of
the year with a suitcase, the barometer, and one hundred and nine
reasons to be glad of the break in the climb.

## The Middle of the Bay

Halfway across, the engine stopped, on schedule and without drama. The
boatman ate a sandwich. The keeper read the barometer, which was lying,
and the sky, which was not, and signalled to the south tower with a
hand lantern until the engine remembered its work.
