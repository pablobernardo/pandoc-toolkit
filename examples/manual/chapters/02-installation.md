# Installation

The unit arrives with the lens capped, a folded card, and nothing else. The card
is the whole installation procedure; this chapter is the same procedure with the
reasons attached.

## Requirements

| Item | Requirement | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Surface | Flat, 40 × 25 cm minimum | The case flexes on uneven surfaces and the lens housing is machined to 0.1 mm |
| Power | 12 V, 1.5 A, centre positive | Under-voltage produces `E-04` intermittently, which wastes an afternoon |
| Light | Any, excluding direct sun on the intake | The intake reads ambient light to decide the display brightness |
| Network | None | The unit has no radio, by design |

## Procedure

1. Uncap the lens and keep the cap. It is the only part that is easy to lose and
   impossible to replace.
2. Seat the unit so that the intake vents face away from you.
3. Connect power **before** the lens, in that order.
4. Wait for the self-test. It takes eleven seconds and ends with a single tone.

### If the self-test fails

A failed self-test prints a code and stops. Do not retry more than twice: the
codes are deterministic and a third attempt tells you what the second already did.

#### Torque note for the mounting screws

The case accepts M4 screws at 0.6 N·m. Above 0.9 N·m the insert spins and the
repair requires opening the case, which voids nothing but costs an hour.

## What installation does not require

There is no calibration step, no registration, no account, and no first-run
interview. A unit that asks you questions on first boot is a unit that will ask
again later.
