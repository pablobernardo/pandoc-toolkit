# Glossary

**Hour Rock** — the islet where the light stands, uncovered at low
water and proud of it.

**The register** — the nightly log of the station. Weather first,
traffic second, lamp third; the order has never been explained
because it has never been questioned.

**Watch** — a period of duty in the lamp room, four hours in this
station, timed by a clock that runs slow and is corrected nightly.
