# The Harbour

The ferry left before dawn, when the town still smelled of bread and
diesel. Mara stood at the rail with her one suitcase and watched the
lamps of the seafront go out one by one, as if the town were closing
its eyes to make her departure easier.

The boatman did not talk. He looked at the water, at the sky, and at
the water again, and the three hours passed in the sound of the engine.

## The Ticket Office

The ticket for Hour Rock was issued from a window with no sign. The
clerk wrote the destination by hand, twice, and made Mara sign the
second copy. Nobody had explained that the second copy stayed behind.

## The Crossing

By noon the coast had flattened into a grey line. The keeper's rock
appeared first as a disturbance in the water, then as a tower, then as
a place with a jetty, a crane and a man waiting with his hands in the
pockets of an oilcoat.
