# The Light

The lamp was a large lens in a small room, and Elias spoke of it the
way other men speak of a family member who has outlived all their
friends. He taught Mara the evening routine in one continuous
demonstration: the wick, the mercury bath, the turning gear, the log.

## The Register

Every night the register recorded the weather, the passing traffic and
the state of the lamp, in that order, in the same hand since 1946.
Mara's first entry sat under thirty years of Elias's entries like a
new plank in an old floor.

## The Watch

The watch was four hours on, four hours off. Elias claimed the human
mind needed exactly four hours of darkness to stay honest. Mara
suspected the rule had more to do with the lamp than with the mind,
and kept her suspicion out of the register.
