# The Storm

The storm arrived on a Tuesday without an appointment. By evening the
jetty was under water and the supply boat had turned back, and the
tower had become the only address on the rock.

## The Glass

The barometer fell through the morning and kept falling, in the
deliberate way of an instrument that is not in a hurry. Elias read it
at each watch change and wrote the same two words: *still falling*.

## The Lamp

They kept the light through the night. The turning gear seized at
three in the morning, and for an hour the two of them rotated the
lens by hand, one quarter turn at a time, while the sea worked on the
rock below with the patience of a locksmith.

In the morning the storm was gone. The register recorded the weather,
the traffic and the state of the lamp, in that order, and Mara wrote
her second entry in a steady hand.
