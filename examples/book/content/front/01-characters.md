\chapter*{The Characters}

\textbf{Mara Voss}, assistant keeper of the Hour Rock light, keeps the
register and the watch rotation in the same worn notebook.

\textbf{Elias Voss}, her uncle and principal keeper, has tended the lamp
for thirty-one winters and trusts no chart drawn after his twentieth.

\textbf{The supply boat} calls twice a month, weather permitting, and is
the only connection between the rock and the harbour town.
